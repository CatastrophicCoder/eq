Spectral Fault @VERSION@

A 16-band parametric and dynamic equalizer by Catastrophic Audio, for Macs with Apple silicon (macOS 12 or later). On the next pages you can choose which formats to install: Audio Unit, VST3 and the Standalone app.

First use: allow it once

This build is not signed with an Apple Developer ID, so macOS asks before running it the first time. If your host skips the plug-in or macOS reports it cannot be checked, open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to Security and click Open Anyway next to Spectral Fault. Then restart the host and rescan plug-ins. In Logic Pro, the plug-in appears as Catastrophic Audio: Spectral Fault.
